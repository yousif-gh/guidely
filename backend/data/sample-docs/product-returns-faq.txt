Product Returns FAQ

What is the return window?
Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they're unused and in original packaging. Items returned between 31 and 60 days qualify for store credit only.

When are refunds issued after a return?
Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse. Refunds go back to the original payment method; store credit is applied instantly.

Do I need the original receipt?
An order confirmation email or order number is sufficient — a printed receipt is not required. Orders can be looked up by email address at returns.company.com.

Who pays for return shipping?
Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat 3 BHD shipping fee is deducted from the refund unless you have a Premium membership.

Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
Yes, exchanges for a different size or color are processed as a return plus a new order, and are not subject to the return shipping fee.

What items are non-returnable?
Personal care items, opened software licenses, and items marked "final sale" at checkout cannot be returned.

My item arrived damaged — what do I do?
Contact support within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. Damaged items are replaced or refunded in full, including original shipping cost, with no return shipping fee.

How do I track my return?
Once your return is scanned by the carrier, tracking updates appear in your account under Orders > Returns within 24 hours.
