Remote Work Policy

Purpose
This policy defines how employees may work remotely while keeping teams productive and data secure. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

Eligibility
Employees become eligible for remote work after completing their 3-month probation period. Roles that require physical presence, such as warehouse operations and front-desk reception, are excluded. Managers approve remote arrangements on a case-by-case basis.

Schedule
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, during which everyone must be reachable on Slack and email. Any change to a standing remote schedule requires 1 week of notice to the direct manager.

Equipment and Expenses
The company provides a laptop and one external monitor for remote work. Employees may claim up to 25 BHD per month for home internet costs through the expense portal. Office chairs and desks are not reimbursable.

Security Requirements
Remote employees must connect through the company VPN at all times, enable full-disk encryption, and lock their screens when away. Working from public Wi-Fi without the VPN is prohibited. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours.

Time Zones and Travel
Working remotely from another country requires written approval from both the manager and HR at least 2 weeks in advance, and is limited to 30 days per calendar year.
