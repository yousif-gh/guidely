Larkfield Supplies Employee FAQ

How many vacation days do I get?
Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days per calendar year. Part-time employees receive a share based on their contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year and must be used by March 31.

How do I report sick leave?
Message your manager before 9 am on the first day you are absent. A doctor's note is needed for absences longer than 3 working days.

Can I work from home?
Office staff may work from home up to 3 days per week after agreeing a schedule with their manager. Warehouse roles must be on site.

When is payday?
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month. Payslips appear in the HR portal two days earlier.

How do I claim expenses?
Submit receipts in the finance portal within 30 days of spending the money. Claims above 500 dollars need written approval from a director before the purchase is made. Approved claims are paid with the next salary.

Who do I contact about a payroll mistake?
Email payroll@larkfield.example with your employee number and the month affected. Payroll replies within 2 working days.

What is the parental leave allowance?
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of paid leave and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Employees qualify after 12 months of service.

Does the company pay for training?
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,000 dollars per year. Your manager must approve the course before you book it.
