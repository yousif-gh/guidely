Vacation and Leave Policy

Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 22 paid vacation days per year, credited monthly at a rate of 1.83 days. Vacation requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance for periods under a week, and 3 weeks in advance for anything longer than 10 consecutive days. Unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year up to a maximum of 10 days; anything beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires payout.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 14 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for any sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not need to be requested in advance, but employees should notify their manager before their normal start time on the day of absence.

Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year based on the employee's home country calendar. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, it is not automatically shifted to the nearest weekday unless stated otherwise in the regional addendum.

Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date and can be split into two blocks with manager approval.

Unpaid Leave
Employees with at least 1 year of tenure may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons, subject to manager and HR approval. Unpaid leave requests must be submitted at least 4 weeks in advance.

Requesting Time Off
All leave requests go through the HR portal at hr.company.com/leave. Approval typically takes 1-2 business days. Employees can check their remaining balance for each leave type at any time from their HR dashboard.
