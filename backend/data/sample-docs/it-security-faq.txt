IT Security FAQ

How do I reset my password?
Go to login.company.com/reset, enter your work email, and follow the link sent to your inbox. Reset links expire after 15 minutes. If you don't receive an email within a few minutes, check your spam folder before contacting IT.

What if I lose my laptop or phone?
Report it to IT within 24 hours by emailing security@company.com or calling the IT hotline. IT will remotely wipe the device if it's enrolled in mobile device management. Do not wait until the next business day to report a loss.

Can I use public Wi-Fi?
Only when connected through the company VPN. Public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited for accessing company systems, email, or internal tools.

How often do I need to change my password?
Passwords must be rotated every 90 days. The system will prompt you 7 days before expiry. Reused passwords from your last 5 passwords are rejected.

What counts as multi-factor authentication (MFA)?
The company authenticator app is the required MFA method for all internal systems. SMS-based codes are accepted only as a fallback and must be registered with IT in advance.

I received a suspicious email — what do I do?
Do not click any links or download attachments. Forward the email to phishing@company.com and delete it. IT reviews all reports within one business day.

Can I install personal software on my work laptop?
Only software approved on the internal software catalog. Requests for anything else go through the IT request portal and typically take 2-3 business days to review.

What happens if I fail a phishing simulation test?
You'll be enrolled in a short mandatory security refresher course. Repeated failures (3 or more in a year) are flagged to your manager.
