Incident Response Guide

Severity Levels
SEV1 means a full outage or data breach affecting all customers; response begins within 15 minutes. SEV2 is a major feature outage affecting many customers; response begins within 1 hour. SEV3 covers minor bugs and degraded performance; response begins within 1 business day.

Declaring an Incident
Any engineer can declare an incident by posting in the #incidents Slack channel with the /incident command. The on-call engineer becomes the incident commander by default until someone more senior takes over explicitly.

Roles
The incident commander coordinates the response and makes final calls. The communications lead posts status updates every 30 minutes for SEV1 and every 2 hours for SEV2. The scribe keeps a timeline of actions and decisions in the incident document.

Communication
Customer-facing status updates are published on the status page. Never share customer data, logs, or screenshots in public channels. Legal must review any external statement about a suspected data breach before it is published.

Resolution and Postmortems
An incident is resolved when the service is fully restored and monitored as stable for 30 minutes. A blameless postmortem is written within 3 business days for every SEV1 and SEV2, and reviewed in the weekly engineering meeting. Action items get owners and due dates in the tracker.
