Larkfield Supplies New Hire Onboarding

Before your first day

HR sends your contract and a checklist two weeks before you start. Return the signed contract and a copy of your ID at least 5 days before your start date.

First day

Arrive at 9:30 am and ask reception for your badge. Your manager meets you at reception and introduces your team. IT gives you a laptop and helps you set up multi-factor authentication before lunch.

First week

Complete the safety course and the security awareness course in the training portal by Friday. Each course takes about 45 minutes. Warehouse staff also complete a half-day practical session with a shift lead.

Your buddy

Every new hire is paired with a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Your buddy is there for everyday questions and should meet you at least once a week during your first month.

Check-ins

Your manager holds check-in meetings at 30, 60 and 90 days. The probation period lasts 6 months, and either side can end employment during it with 2 weeks of notice. After probation the notice period is 2 months.

Getting help

HR questions go to hr@larkfield.example. Technical problems go to the IT help desk at extension 4400.
