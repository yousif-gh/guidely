New Employee Onboarding Guide

Week 1: Getting Set Up
On your first day you will receive your laptop, badge, and accounts. Complete the security awareness training in the learning portal within your first 3 days. Your manager will assign you an onboarding buddy who is your first point of contact for day-to-day questions.

Accounts and Tools
You will get access to email, Slack, the HR portal, and your team project tracker. Access to production systems is not granted during the first 2 weeks and requires a separate access request approved by your team lead.

Week 2 to 4: Learning the Ropes
Schedule 30-minute intro calls with each member of your immediate team. Read your team runbook and shadow at least two support rotations. By the end of week 4 you should complete your first small task end-to-end.

Payroll and Benefits
Salaries are paid on the 25th of each month. Health insurance activates from day one; dependents can be added within the first 30 days through the HR portal. The company matches pension contributions up to 5% of base salary.

Probation and Reviews
The probation period lasts 3 months and ends with a review covering goals, feedback, and confirmation of employment. Your manager will set 2 to 3 measurable goals with you during your first week.
