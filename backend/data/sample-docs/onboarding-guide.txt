Onboarding Guide

Purpose
This guide walks new hires through their first two weeks, from account setup to first team meeting.

Before Day One
HR sends a welcome email with your start date, manager's name, and a checklist link 5 business days before you start. IT provisions your company email and laptop by end of day on your start date.

Day One
Arrive by 9:00 for orientation, which covers company overview, benefits enrollment, and a building/office tour. You'll receive your laptop, access badge, and login credentials. Complete mandatory compliance training (data privacy and security basics) by end of week one.

First Week
Meet 1:1 with your manager to review your 30/60/90-day goals. Shadow a teammate on at least two real tasks. Join your team's recurring meetings and Slack channels. Set up your development environment (or relevant tools for your role) with help from your onboarding buddy.

Systems Access
Standard access (email, Slack, HR portal) is granted automatically on day one. Role-specific system access (e.g. production systems, finance tools) requires manager approval and is typically granted within 3 business days.

30-Day Check-In
Your manager schedules a formal check-in at the 30-day mark to review progress against onboarding goals and address any blockers.

90-Day Review
A more thorough performance and fit conversation happens at 90 days, marking the end of the standard onboarding period. Probation status (where applicable) is confirmed or extended at this point.

Who to Contact
IT issues: it-support@company.com. HR/benefits questions: hr@company.com. General onboarding questions: your assigned onboarding buddy or manager.
