Larkfield Supplies Returns, Refunds and Shipping Policy

Purpose

This policy explains how customer returns, refunds, exchanges and shipping claims are handled at Larkfield Supplies. Support staff should follow it for every order. Managers may approve an exception only when it is recorded on the customer ticket with a reason.

Return window

Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery. The 30 days start on the delivery date shown by the carrier, not on the order date. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. A receipt or order number is required for every return. Without either one, support staff may offer store credit only, and only up to 50 dollars.

Items that cannot be returned

Custom printed products, opened ink cartridges, gift cards and clearance items marked final sale cannot be returned. If one of these items arrives damaged, the damaged goods section of this policy still applies.

Refund timing

Refunds take 14 days from the date the warehouse receives the returned item, unless the item is damaged. Refunds for damaged items take 3 business days and do not wait for the item to reach the warehouse. Refunds always go back to the original payment method. If that card has been closed, the customer receives store credit for the full amount.

Damaged goods

Customers must report damage within 7 days of delivery and include at least one photo of the item and one photo of the packaging. Support staff approve damage claims under 200 dollars without manager review. Claims of 200 dollars or more need approval from a support manager. The customer can choose a replacement or a refund. Larkfield pays return shipping for all damaged items.

Exchanges

An exchange for a different size or color is free once per order. A second exchange on the same order costs a flat fee of 6 dollars. Exchanges ship only after the warehouse has received the original item, unless the customer pays for the replacement first and is refunded later.

Return shipping costs

Customers pay return shipping unless the item was damaged or Larkfield sent the wrong item. Standard return labels cost 8 dollars and the amount is deducted from the refund. Customers may use their own carrier, but Larkfield is not responsible for parcels lost on the way back without tracking.

Standard shipping

Orders placed before 2 pm local time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 15 dollars. Orders over 75 dollars ship free using standard delivery.

Lost parcels

A parcel is treated as lost when tracking has not changed for 10 business days. Support staff open a carrier claim and send a replacement at no cost without waiting for the claim to finish.

International orders

Larkfield ships to Canada and the United Kingdom only. International customers pay any customs duties. Duties are not refunded when an order is returned.

Policy owner

The Head of Customer Support owns this policy and reviews it every January.
