Expense and Reimbursement Policy

General Rules
All business expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of being incurred. Each claim needs an itemized receipt; credit card statements alone are not accepted. Claims are approved by the direct manager and paid with the next payroll cycle.

Meals and Client Entertainment
Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to 20 BHD per day. Client entertainment requires pre-approval from a department head and is capped at 40 BHD per person. Alcohol is not reimbursable under any category.

Travel
Economy class is the default for flights under 6 hours; business class may be approved for longer flights. Hotels are capped at 60 BHD per night in the GCC and 90 BHD per night elsewhere. Ride-hailing and taxi fares for business trips are fully reimbursable with receipts.

Software and Subscriptions
Individual software subscriptions above 10 BHD per month require IT approval before purchase to avoid duplicate tooling. Annual renewals are reviewed each January by the finance team.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal entertainment, traffic fines, minibar purchases, and expenses without receipts are never reimbursed. Submitting a fraudulent claim is grounds for disciplinary action.
