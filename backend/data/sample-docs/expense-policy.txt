Expense Policy

Purpose
This policy explains how employees submit, approve, and get reimbursed for business expenses. It applies to all full-time and part-time staff.

Submission
Expenses must be submitted through the expense portal within 30 days of the purchase date. Each claim requires an itemized receipt; claims without a receipt over 10 BHD are rejected automatically.

Approval
Claims under 100 BHD are auto-approved after manager sign-off. Claims between 100 and 500 BHD require both manager and department head approval. Anything above 500 BHD needs finance team pre-approval before the purchase is made.

Categories and Limits
Meals while traveling are capped at 15 BHD per day. Local transport (taxi, ride-share) is reimbursable in full with a receipt. Client entertainment is capped at 50 BHD per person and requires the client's name and purpose in the claim notes.

Travel
Flights must be booked through the corporate travel portal at least 7 days in advance when possible. Economy class is standard; business class is only approved for flights over 6 hours, with department head sign-off. Hotel stays are capped at 120 BHD per night in major cities.

Reimbursement Timeline
Approved claims are paid out with the next payroll cycle, typically within 10 business days of approval. Rejected claims include a reason and can be resubmitted once corrected.

Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (outside approved client entertainment), personal subscriptions, traffic fines, and upgrades to premium seating are not reimbursable under any circumstance.
